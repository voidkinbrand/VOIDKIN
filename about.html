DOCUMENT A-01FOUR NOTES ON THE SUBJECT
+

VOIDKIN is a clothing label concerned with what is usually filed away: anxiety, isolation, and the strange things we buy to feel in control.

The void is not a romantic destination. It is the space between what people expect and what it feels like to be there.

Our graphics borrow the language of notices, advertisements and ordinary objects. A face, a shopping cart, a rat-control announcement. The unsettling part is how familiar they look.

These are garments, not solutions. Their purpose is to make an observation and leave the record open.
