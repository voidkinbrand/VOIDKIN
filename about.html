ABOUT
THE VOID.

An archive disguised as a clothing label. Or the other way around.

01 / CONTEXT

NO BRAND MYTH REQUIRED.

VOIDKIN makes garments about anxiety, isolation and consumer absurdity. The themes are familiar. The presentation is deliberately administrative.

Here, an image becomes a case file. An item gets a number. A feeling is filed under a category that does not explain it.

The word “void” is not a promise of depth. It names the space between what is happening and the version of it we are expected to accept.

These are clothes. The ideas stay attached to them, even after the sale.
